## English translation

Dear Mr. Jean-Pierre Taro Corbeil,

Thank you for your email inquiry.

Mitsugi District (御調郡), Hiroshima Prefecture, is now split across parts of **Onomichi City, Mihara City, Fuchū City, and Sera Town (Sera District)**.

"Mitsugi District" alone doesn't tell us whether the place is now part of Onomichi, so **you'll need to identify the village or town name**. Once you have it, we can check whether it is now part of Onomichi and let you know.

If the registered domicile (*honseki*, 本籍地) turns out to be in Onomichi, you can request the family register (*koseki*). You will need:

1. **Request form** (you can download it from the Onomichi City website)
2. **Fee:** ¥750 per copy (for a *joseki tōhon*, 除籍謄本: a full copy of a closed family register)
3. **Proof of identity** (My Number card, driver's licence, etc.)
4. **Birth certificate(s)** showing how you are related to your great-grandfather
   - The birth certificates must come with **Japanese translations**

You can also apply by mail. If you do, send the documents above (**photocopies** of your ID and birth certificates are fine) together with a **stamped, self-addressed return envelope**. Please pay the fee with a **postal money order** (*teigaku kogawase*, 定額小為替) bought at a Japan Post office.

If you have any other questions, please contact us.

Onomichi City Hall, Citizens' Life Department, Citizens Division
1-15-1 Kubo, Onomichi, Hiroshima 722-8501
Tel. 0848-38-9150

## Key terms

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 御調郡 | Mitsugi-gun | Mitsugi District (historical district, dissolved) |
| 本籍地 | honsekichi | Registered domicile on the family register. It can differ from the birthplace |
| 戸籍 | koseki | Family register |
| 除籍謄本 | joseki tōhon | Full copy of a closed register (everyone listed has died or moved off it) |
| 改製原戸籍 | kaisei genkoseki | Pre-reformat original register (older format, often holds older generations) |
| 曾祖父 | sōsofu | Great-grandfather (here: JP's great-grandfather = Mom's grandfather) |
| 続柄 | zokugara | Family relationship |
| 定額小為替 | teigaku kogawase | Fixed-amount postal money order |
